# Baby Steps

**Extending the biological clock.**

[Open the interactive presentation](https://franz23.github.io/baby-steps/)

Baby Steps is a hackathon research project exploring how better evidence could inform embryo-screening and fertility decisions. The presentation connects three contributions:

- **Lab reporting benchmarks:** compare published mosaic-call rates across laboratories within age groups, making differences in reporting visible.
- **An embryo-screening test bench:** analyze existing biopsy data, compare one biopsy with two, check results against inner-cell samples from donated embryos, and evaluate the trade-offs.
- **Immune-signalling replication:** test whether an earlier immune-to-stromal signalling pattern associated with recurrent implantation failure holds in an independent dataset. The pattern did not replicate; more data across populations are needed.

## What this repository contains

This repository contains the eight-slide presentation and its media assets. It does **not** contain the underlying analysis pipeline, a runnable scientific test bench, or raw research datasets. The embedded explorer is an illustrative animation, not a live diagnostic tool.

## Evidence and scope

Sources include published laboratory age tables, donated-embryo research and the single-cell datasets GSE183837 and GSE250130. Specific sources and methodological caveats appear in slide footers and speaker notes.

Mosaic-call rates describe reporting behaviour, not laboratory quality or the probability of a healthy birth. Agreement with sampled inner cells is not proof of clinical safety. Immune signalling is inferred from RNA, and the replication does not establish a validated predictor of implantation outcomes.

This is a research presentation, not medical advice or a clinically validated screening product.
